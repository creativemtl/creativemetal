Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 June 2026

This privacy policy explains how Creative Metal handles personal information when you contact us through this website, by phone, by email, or through WhatsApp.

Information We Collect

We may collect the personal information you choose to send to us, such as your name, phone number, email address, location, project details, photos, drawings, or other information needed to respond to your enquiry.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to enquiries, discuss project requirements, prepare quotes, arrange site visits, provide services, and keep basic records relating to work requested or completed.

Cookies and Tracking

This website does not use cookies, advertising trackers, analytics trackers, or similar tracking technologies.

Sharing Information

We do not sell personal information. We only share information where it is reasonably necessary to provide a service, comply with the law, or work with trusted service providers involved in the project.

Keeping Information Safe

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from loss, misuse, unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Your Choices

You may ask us to correct, update, or delete personal information we hold about you, subject to any legal or business record-keeping requirements.
